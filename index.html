We made 11 versions of the page where you pick your ride. Please try each one and tell us what you think. It takes about 10 minutes.

Imagine: you're flying from Venice Airport. You need a ride from Ljubljana on Thu 17 Sep that gets you there by 10:15. On each version, look at the options and pick the ride you'd book, then tap Continue to rate that version.

For each version we ask 3 quick questions: is it clear, is it easy, do you like it. There are no wrong answers, we're testing the pages, not you.
